# 2.3 Project Scope

**What the System Will D0**

The ConnectHub system will:

**1)** Give individual career ladder suggestions to new entrants.

**2)** Pair users with employment, gatherings, and volunteering and accreditation.

**3)** Given the option of creating profile according to background, experience and preferences.

**4)** Provide career advice rather than job postings.

**5)** Filter opportunities by their diversity and background.

**6**) Process job applications via a combined application portal.

**7)** Assist the users to find community events and networking.

**8)** Store and maintain user and opportunity information.

**What the System Will NOT DO**

The system of the connectHub will not:

**1)** Assure employment or hiring.

**2)** Offer immigration, legal, or visa counsel.

**3)** Substitute internal hiring systems by employers.

**4)** Provide financial support or government subsidies.

**5)** Perform as a social media/messaging platform.

**6)** Offer real time scheduling of interviews or human resource management systems.

# 2.4 System Risks

The development and implementation of ConnectHub involve several risks that may potentially impact the project's success. These risks have been categorized and evaluated based on its likelihood of occurrence and its potential impact on the project.

| **Risk Category** | **Risk Description** | **Probability** | **Impact** | **Overall Risk** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Market | Low user adoption due to limited awareness among newcomers | Medium | High | High |
| Market | Competition from existing job platforms reducing engagement | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Financial | Limited funding or budget constraints affecting development progress | Medium | High | High |
| Financial | Unexpected costs related to infrastructure or software services | Low | Medium | Medium |
| Technology | Difficulties in developing an accurate recommendation system | Medium | High | High |
| Technology | System performance issues with increased user traffic | Low | Medium | Medium |
| People | Team members lacking experience with certain technologies | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| People | Delays caused by workload or scheduling conflicts | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| Structure/Process | Poor communication within the team leading to development delays | Low | Medium | Medium |
| Structure/Process | Changes in project scope affecting timelines and deliverables | Medium | High | High |

**Risk Management Approach**

To manage these risks, the team will follow standard project risk management practices. This includes regularly identifying new risks, evaluating any existing ones, and implementing mitigation strategies. Contingency plans can be developed for high-impact risks, such as budgeting reserves for unexpected expenses and additional testing phases to address technical challenges. Our weekly team meetings conducted will frequently monitor the progress and ensure effective communication.

# 2.5 Operating Environment

ConnectHub is designed as a web and mobile-friendly application to ensure accessibility for a wide range of users.  
<br/>**Hardware and Devices Supported**

- Smartphones and tablets (Android & IOS)
- Desktop and laptop computers
- Minimum recommended device requirements
  - 2GB RAM
  - Modern web browser or mobile device

**Operating Systems**

- Android 10 or higher
- iOS 13 or higher
- Windows 10 or higher
- macOS 11 or higher

**Software Dependencies**

- Web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Safari, or Firefox)
- Backend server environment (such as Node.js or similar framework)
- Database system (such as MongoDB or relational database)
- Cloud hosting services for scalability and data storage

**Internet Connectivity**

- An active internet connection is required for:
  - User authentication
  - Accessing event and training data
  - Recommendation updates
  - Real-time content synchronization
- Limited offline functionality may be available for viewing previously loaded content, but core features will require internet access.
