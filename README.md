# Project Name

# Project Summary
Background
  Our company feels as if newcomers into any city or country find it harder to start their careers rather than easier after moving or applying for a work permit. Many high level experienced newcomers still remain unemployed or are unsure on what career they would like to pursue. Therefore our company has a mission to create a application for the purpose of a personalized career-navigation platform.

Description
  Company Value Add
    -	A more advanced and engaging application over competitors
    -	More than application portal
    -	Reduce the unemployed newcomer percentage
    -	All inclusive and also finding a specific diversity setting
  End-User Value Add
    -	Newcomers have a guided pathway
    -	They have a chance to switch careers
    -	More newcomers who have high level experience get hired in their respected career path
    -	Finding others from the same background. 
    -	Helping users feel like they are not thrown in the dark
Scope
  What is Included
    -	Job event postings
    -	Searches for careers and pathways related to certification
    -	Application portal
    -	Categorizes based on background preferences
    -	Training related to career
  What is Not Included

Justification
  The justifications to this is that there are high rates of newcomers that are unemployed and that struggle looking for jobs that are in their field or jobs in general making it harder for them to search and having to apply multiple times to different areas. This causes a overreliances on the job board that have a low response or acceptance rate which makes it harder. Which then newcomers have a high demand of mentorship, local experience, and guidance based platforms in which we would like to develop.
