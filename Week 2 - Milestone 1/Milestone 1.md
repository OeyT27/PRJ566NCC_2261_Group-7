# 2.1 Project Proposal

## 2.1.1 Project Background

Newcomers to Toronto often arrive with valuable international education and work experience, yet struggle to translate it into local opportunities. The problem is not a lack of skill - it is a lack of guidance, visibility, and structured pathways. Job boards are overcrowded, volunteer opportunities are scattered, and training or certification requirements are unclear. As a result, many newcomers waste time applying blindly, miss experience-building opportunities, or accept work far below their qualifications.

## 2.1.2 Problem Statement

The problem that our company sees is that when newcomers arrive in a new city they often times are unaware of how to convert international experience into local career opportunities. Platforms such as LinkdIn and Indeed are complicated, often times unprofessional, and assume that the user has prior knowledge to the local job market. The proof shows that there are still skilled professionals who still remain unemployed or apply for a job that does not corelate to their experience and other newcomers have expressed frustration and delayed growth towards their career.

## 2.1.3 Product Vision

The vision our group has is that we develop an application that provide newcomers with recommendations for the career that they have experience in and help provide events, volunteer, training, and certification that can help build their career. This also reduces the amount of trial and error for job application since many companies tend to reject due to inflation or unwanted employees with no local experience. The way that this is different than other job application services is that we allow the feature to look for a specific group so that there is an increased chance of both new hires and feeling at home with the people from the same background.
